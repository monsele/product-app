# UI Design Brief

## Product

An AI-powered learning platform that turns a teacher’s written material into
visual, motion-graphics learning experiences.

## Primary users

1. Teachers creating and reviewing lessons
2. Students consuming visual lessons

## Design direction

- Premium creative-learning product, not a generic SaaS admin dashboard
- Clean typography and generous spacing
- Light dashboard and lesson pages
- Dark storyboard / scene editing workspace
- Navy or charcoal foundation with one vivid accent: electric blue, violet, or teal
- Use cards sparingly; prioritize hierarchy and canvas space
- Accessible contrast, clear labels, visible loading/error/empty states

## Reference patterns

- Runway: generation status and creative workspace density
- Figma: left navigation, central canvas, right inspector layout
- Brilliant: lesson progression and visual-first teaching
- Canva: approachable creation flows

## Do not do

- Do not use a generic template-dashboard look
- Do not overuse gradients, glassmorphism, or excessive rounded cards
- Do not make the editor feel crowded
- Do not invent functionality that does not exist
